# Evaluation Prompt: Devil's Advocate

## Objective
Stress-test a top-ranked idea cluster by constructing the strongest possible objections and assessing the idea's resilience.

## Cluster Information
- **Cluster ID**: [Populate per cluster]
- **Core Idea**: [One-sentence description]
- **Current Ranking**: [Position in scoring matrix]
- **Key Strengths**: [From prior evaluation steps]

## Process

### Step 1: Identify Attack Vectors
Consider objections from multiple angles:
- **Technical**: Fundamental technical barriers or impossibilities
- **Practical**: Resource, timeline, or operational obstacles
- **Competitive**: Why someone else would do this better or has already done it
- **Structural**: Misalignment with the original problem or unintended consequences
- **Evidence**: Weaknesses in the supporting evidence or reasoning

### Step 2: Construct Top 3 Objections
For each objection:
- State the argument clearly and forcefully
- Provide evidence or reasoning supporting the objection
- Assess severity: minor concern / significant challenge / potential fatal flaw

### Step 3: Counter-Arguments
For each objection:
- Provide the strongest possible counter-argument or mitigation strategy
- Identify what evidence would be needed to resolve the concern
- Assess confidence in the counter-argument: strong / moderate / weak

## Expected Output
- 3 strongest objections with counter-arguments
- Fatal flaw probability: low / medium / high
- Overall resilience assessment: strong / moderate / fragile
- Recommended actions: proceed as-is / proceed with modifications / reconsider
